Understand the result

Benchmarks describe the measured setup and metric. Deployment checks describe the URLs and artifacts they actually inspected. File-size encoding can change video quality. CAD previews can omit unsupported entities. Asset budgets are not frame-rate predictions. The tools expose these limits rather than guarantee a universal result.

Keep your originals

Review plans before applying file changes and retain independent backups of important material. Safe Tidy receipts can undo unchanged moves; they do not replace backups. Generated files and reports remain under your control.

Support and changes

Report reproducible problems through the support page. Release versions and checksums identify the package you installed. Review release notes before upgrading workflows you rely on.
